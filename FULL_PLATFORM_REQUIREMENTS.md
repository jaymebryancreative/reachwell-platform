# Reachwell Full Platform Requirements

## People & Profiles
### Identity
- First, middle, last and preferred name
- Photo/avatar
- Birthday and calculated age
- Pronouns only if the organization chooses to collect them
- Marital status
- Household/family relationships

### Contact
- Email(s), phone(s), address(es)
- Preferred contact method and communication preferences
- Emergency contact

### Engagement
- Teams and ministry groups
- Roles and permissions
- Skills, interests and availability
- Serving history and event history
- Attendance and sign-in history
- Follow-ups and authorized care/outreach records
- Communication history

### Actions
- Create, view, edit, archive/restore and authorized export
- Search, filters, tags and saved views
- Assign/remove teams and roles
- Schedule for events
- Message, email and create follow-up

## Teams & Ministry Groups
- Built-in and custom teams
- Leaders, coordinators and members
- Team roster and availability
- Team channel and announcements
- Event assignments and schedules
- Team projects/tasks
- Team progress and serving history

## Communication
- Organization announcements
- Team channels
- Direct and group conversations
- Event and project/task conversations
- Search, history, read state, notifications and mentions
- Authorized attachments/files
- Email composition, delivery provider, delivery history and preferences

## Events & Sign-In
- Event creation/editing, dates, locations and details
- Setup teams and custom assignments
- Rosters and scheduling
- Event channel
- Check-in/sign-in
- Present/absent/late/custom statuses and absence reasons
- Attendance history and follow-up

## Mission Mode
- Touch-friendly switch and field layout
- Assignments/routes
- Note/Need/Prayer records with independent persistence
- Completion and follow-up
- Overall and team progress with percentages
- Responsive phone/tablet operation

## Workspace
- Projects, boards/lists and simple statuses
- Tasks, assignees, due dates and priorities
- Team and event connections
- Contextual discussion

## Giving & Finance
- Donor records and giving history
- Funds/designations and receipts
- Payment integrations through approved providers
- Budgets, accounts, categories, spending and reconciliation workflows
- Restricted permissions and audit history
- Reports, print and export

## Settings & Administration
- Organization profile
- Members/invitations
- Roles and granular permissions
- Teams and custom groups
- Communication and notification settings
- Security/recovery
- Integrations
- Subscription and billing administration
- Data/export/retention controls

## Files & Data Continuity
- Upload, organization ownership and authorized deletion
- Access controls and audit history
- Export/print key records
- Subscription cancellation/non-renewal continuity workflow
- Retention and recovery policies

## AI Help
- Context-aware platform assistance
- Permission-aware answers
- Safe recommendations and escalation paths

## Quality Gates
Every production feature requires: create, read, update, appropriate delete/archive behavior, validation, organization isolation, server-side authorization, persistence, responsive design and meaningful error states.
